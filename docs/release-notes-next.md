## Beta notes

Beta for testers. If you are not beta testing, stay on v2.0.

The editor has a new layout.

- Layers come first in the left column, and one Add button opens a sheet with every element, preset and saved part on an All tab. Press / to open it.
- Pages are tiles with their layer count and their own trash can. Four buttons under them add a Previous page, Next page, One page or Play all pages tap zone, and land on the new layer with its settings lit.
- The row above the face holds the name (click it to rename), the devices the design is on, and Add to a device. Duplicate and Delete are icons at the right, with History under the ··· menu.
- Delete asks in a dialog that says where the design is, that it cannot be undone, and how to keep it: the x on a device chip takes it off that device, and the last copy goes to Unassigned.
- One floating toolbar over the face: Taps, Demo, one Preview as menu for case size and color, Snap, and the zoom.
- Every settings card opens on a new selection. Collapse all folds back to Content and Look, and a folded card says its settings in one line.
- Pick mode is gone. Rest the pointer on a row in Layers to see where that layer sits on the face.
- Tap layers are called tap zones. A watch is named on its own, without the paired iPhone in parentheses.

Also since beta.10:

- The tap menu explains every action, in groups. Show one page and Open an entity are new tap actions.
- Page hold times for Play all pages are set under that tap, with a Test on canvas button.
- Menus open above their button when they do not fit below.
- Tinted previews draw sharp in Safari and in the iOS app.
- Timed refresh is called Auto refresh timer. Multiple refresh is marked beta, and refresh taps say how the daily redraw budget works.

Use it with Wrist Assistant 2.8.0 from TestFlight.
